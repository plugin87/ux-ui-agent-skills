A project keeps one client's work in one place

Files, tasks, and the invoice history live together, so nothing has to be reconstructed from email later. Most teams start with the client they are billing this month.

  1. 1Name the project after the client, not the month.
  2. 2Invite the two or three people who actually touch the work.
  3. 3Drop in the files you already have. Nothing is public until you share it.

Takes about a minute. You can rename or delete it afterwards.

Moving from somewhere else?

Import brings across project names, members, and files. Tasks come over as a checklist you can edit.
